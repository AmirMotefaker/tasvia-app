# Tasvin Foundation v1.0

## Product

Tasvin

## Vision

Build the financial operation layer between businesses, suppliers, payments, and accounting systems.

## Mission

Simplify and automate business payment workflows with transparent reconciliation.

## Core Problem

Businesses struggle with:

- Manual card-to-card payments
- Missing payment records
- Invoice-payment mismatch
- Accounting errors
- Lack of financial visibility

## Initial ICP

Primary:

- Restaurants
- Cafes
- Multi-branch businesses

Future:

- Retail businesses
- Service companies
- Enterprise operations

## MVP Scope

### Payment Request

Businesses create structured payment requests.

### Supplier Payment

Payments are linked to suppliers and invoices.

### Payment Verification

Payment evidence and transaction data are verified.

### Reconciliation

Transactions are matched with invoices.

### Reporting

Financial history and operational reports are generated.
